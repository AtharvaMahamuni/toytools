ToyTools

ToyTools is the internet's little toolbox: free, browser-based tools for text, numbers, developer utilities, productivity, money, generators, physics, applied math, chemistry, dates, health, design, music, fidgets, prep, and games. The prep tools prepare text before a model (ToyTools does not run a model). The text, files and values you enter in tools are processed on your device and not uploaded. The site uses analytics, and the feedback form sends what you submit.

ToyTools is one static platform rather than a collection of separate utilities: the computation lives in a set of shared, individually tested engines, and the interface, offline support and privacy contract belong to the platform, so a tool is largely a declaration of which engine it runs on. What it exposes is single-purpose utilities plus interactive physics, applied math, and chemistry simulations, organized into the categories below. Each category page lists its individual tools. Games are hot-seat boards for two people on one device.

Every published tool is listed in llms-full.txt.

Core tools

  • Word Counter: Count words as you type, and set a goal to write to.
  • Character Counter: Count characters, with and without spaces.
  • Find and Replace: Search and replace text, with regex and a live match count.
  • Percentage Calculator: Percent of a number, or the change between two. On your device.
  • Discount Calculator: Sale price and how much you save, by percentage or amount.
  • JSON Formatter: Pretty-print JSON with proper indentation.
  • Base64 Encoder and Decoder: Encode and decode Base64 in your browser.
  • JWT Decoder: Read a JSON Web Token header, payload and claims. Never uploaded.
  • Password Generator: Strong random passwords, with a live strength estimate.
  • UUID Generator: Random version 4 UUIDs, one or many at a time.
  • QR Code Generator: QR codes for a URL, Wi-Fi, contact or plain text. PNG or SVG.
  • Age Calculator: Your exact age in years, months and days, plus a birthday countdown.
  • Unix Timestamp Converter: Unix timestamp to a date and back, in seconds or milliseconds.
  • Timezone Converter: Convert a time between zones, daylight saving handled.
  • BMI Calculator: Your BMI from height and weight, with the healthy range for you.
  • TDEE Calculator: Your daily energy expenditure, and calories to lose or gain.
  • Compound Interest Calculator: Watch savings grow, with optional monthly contributions.
  • SIP Calculator: Future value of a monthly SIP, and what returns add on top.
  • Tip Calculator: The tip, the total, and the split between any number of people.
  • WCAG Color Contrast Checker: Check text and background against WCAG AA and AAA contrast.
  • Scientific Calculator: Trigonometry, logarithms, powers and roots, with a visible history.
  • Notepad: Quick notes that save themselves, right in your browser.
  • Pomodoro Timer: Stay focused with timed work sessions and breaks.
  • SHA-256 Hash Generator: Generate a SHA-256 hash from any text.
  • Lorem Ipsum Generator: Placeholder text by paragraph, sentence or word.

Prep tools

Prepare text before a model. ToyTools does not run a model.

  • Prompt Packer: Assembles a prompt from fields. Does not write it.
  • Context Fit Checker: An estimate of context use. Not a tokenizer.
  • Chat Export Cleaner: Clean a copied chat into plain text.

Categories

  • Text Utilities: Count, transform, clean, and analyze text strings. (32 tools)
  • Number Utilities: Convert, format, and calculate numeric values quickly. (11 tools)
  • Developer Utilities: Encode, decode, format, and inspect data structures. (35 tools)
  • Productivity: Simple tools to help you focus, plan, and get things done. (6 tools)
  • Money and Finance: Calculate interest, savings, inflation, and plan smarter money decisions. (13 tools)
  • Generators: Generate passwords, UUIDs, random strings, placeholder text, and QR codes in your browser. (10 tools)
  • Physics: Interactive physics simulations you can see, touch, and experiment with. Explore waves, oscillations, and heat right in your browser. (12 tools)
  • Applied Math: Solve triangles, multiply matrices, work fractions and factor primes, or drag the unit circle and a parabola to see the math move, all in your browser. (11 tools)
  • Chemistry: Interactive chemistry simulations you can turn, tune, and run. Conformers, bonds, molecular shape, crystal field, and reaction rates. (6 tools)
  • Date and Time: Calculate ages, durations and sleep cycle bedtimes, convert time zones and timestamps, and see when a date can be read two ways, all in your browser. (8 tools)
  • Health and Fitness: Calculate fitness metrics, track your health, and build habits that stick, all privately in your browser. (21 tools)
  • Design and CSS: Convert colors, check WCAG contrast, build OKLCH shade scales and modular type scales, and translate CSS units, all privately in your browser. (10 tools)
  • Music and Audio: Build, understand and share music equalizer settings, then apply them in your own player. (1 tool)
  • Fidgets: Online virtual fidget toys you tap, flick, or flip in the browser, with optional sound and haptics. What you enter stays on your device. (8 tools)
  • Prep for a model: Tools for preparing, cleaning, structuring, and checking text before you send it to an AI model. ToyTools does not run a model. (6 tools)
  • Games: Hot-seat board games for two people on one phone. Pass the device, undo a mis-tap, and rematch without an account. What you enter stays on your device. (4 tools)
